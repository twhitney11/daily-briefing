STORY 01 · SUNY · Higher education

A college transfer pathway comes with a destination already in view

Tompkins Cortland Community College and SUNY Cortland have signed a dual admission agreement beginning with students seeking admission for fall 2027. Students in eligible associate programs who complete their degree with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.5 will be guaranteed admission to SUNY Cortland and transfer with junior standing. Advising from both institutions and access to selected Cortland services give the partnership a practical purpose beyond the admission letter: making the transition between campuses easier to navigate.

STORY 02 · AI · Documents

Document-reading AI trades a general conversation for structured answers

Cloudflare has released updated Clairic and new Clairic-flash models with open weights, focusing on extracting structured information from text, images and PDFs. The system can return fields such as invoice details alongside confidence scores and source spans, while the smaller Flash model has nine billion parameters. This specialization could make routine document processing easier to integrate into software, but Cloudflare explicitly describes its internal performance evaluations as self-reported guidance rather than independent proof.

STORY 03 · Cybersecurity · Enterprise IT

The web server’s enabled features determine its exposure

NHS England has issued an alert covering five NGINX vulnerabilities that can crash worker processes or, under additional conditions, allow unauthenticated attackers to execute code. The affected configurations differ across HTTP/2, HTTP/3, proxy and mail-proxy features, and the alert qualifies code execution as requiring address-space randomization to be disabled or bypassed. CERT-EU reported a successful researcher demonstration but no reported exploitation in the wild at publication, while recommending prompt updates with priority for internet-facing systems. For operators, the useful next step is to match deployed products and enabled features to the vendor’s update guidance, rather than assume every NGINX installation has identical exposure.

STORY 04 · Earth science · Pacific

A November cyclone traces the Pacific’s shifting warm-water risks

NASA’s November 20 Earth Observatory feature documents Cyclone Xavier’s rapid strengthening near the northern Cook Islands, using an Aqua satellite image captured on November 17. The storm developed beside an extensive band of warm Pacific water during El Niño, a pattern associated with a northeastward shift in the region’s cyclone activity. NASA reports that Xavier damaged homes and disrupted power before weakening as it moved west, illustrating why the geography of seasonal risk matters alongside the number of storms. The article cites a regional outlook of 11 to 15 named cyclones for the season; that is a forecast, not a tally of storms that have already occurred.

STORY 05 · Energy · Manufacturing

A battery-material deal moves silicon anodes toward a larger supply chain

Sila has signed a multiyear agreement to supply Panasonic Energy with silicon-carbon anode material from its Moses Lake, Washington plant for battery cells made in the United States. The company describes the broader commercial relationship as potentially exceeding one billion dollars over time, rather than announcing that amount as completed sales. Sila also says supply for its key customers depends on qualification, making this a manufacturing and customer-validation milestone rather than proof that the resulting cells are already shipping. The significance is in connecting a new battery material to an established cell producer, where scaling and dependable supply must catch up with the promise of the chemistry.
