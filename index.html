STORY 01 · SUNY · Campus community

Student leadership turns belonging into a resource students can use

SUNY has named Stony Brook graduate student Michelle Poulos and SUNY ESF undergraduate Brielle Thomas recipients of its third annual Harvey Milk Award for Student Leadership. The award provides a one-time $1,500 non-tuition scholarship and recognizes work to improve campus culture for LGBT+ students. Thomas helped establish ESF’s free Community Closet, gathering donations and campus space for resources including professional attire and winter coats, while Poulos combines campus programming with advocacy and social-work training. Their projects show how student leadership can make inclusion practical by connecting people with everyday resources and a supportive community.

STORY 02 · AI · Quantum computing

An AI’s better quantum algorithm comes with a mathematical check

Google DeepMind and Google Quantum AI report that teams of Gemini 4 agents designed more efficient quantum circuits, with proposed improvements checked in the Lean theorem prover before entering a shared research notebook. For one fusion-simulation calculation, the researchers report an 11.8-fold reduction in a computing-cost measure combining qubits and operations, alongside reduced memory requirements. The result concerns algorithmic resource estimates rather than a demonstrated speedup on today’s quantum hardware, and the team says the scientific simulations still exceed what the first error-corrected quantum computers are likely to support. The broader opportunity is a research workflow in which AI proposes ideas while formal verification checks their mathematical correctness, leaving people to choose problems and interpret the results.

STORY 03 · Cybersecurity · Enterprise IT

The clue to an AI-driven intrusion may be the pace between steps

Microsoft Threat Intelligence says state-sponsored and financially motivated attackers are using ARTEX, an AI-powered penetration-testing framework, to accelerate reconnaissance, credential attacks and web exploitation. The framework combines existing tools and techniques rather than inventing new attack methods, but it can adapt and progress through an intrusion quickly once an attacker defines the targets. Microsoft identifies rapid bursts of varied requests and fast transitions between attack stages as more durable detection clues than easily changed IP addresses or user-agent strings. For enterprise defenders, the report reinforces the value of correlating behavior across systems while reducing exposed services, protecting secrets and restricting routes between machines.

STORY 04 · Space · Orbital logistics

Thanksgiving’s orbital delivery brings supplies—and a future trash run

Roscosmos’s Progress 97 cargo spacecraft docked at the International Space Station’s Rassvet module at 7:08 a.m. Eastern on Thanksgiving Day, NASA confirmed. The uncrewed spacecraft carried about three tons of food, fuel and supplies for the Expedition 75 crew after launching from Kazakhstan on November 24. It is expected to stay for about eight months before leaving for a planned destructive re-entry that will dispose of station trash. The arrival illustrates the two-way logistics behind a continuously occupied research station: incoming essentials keep the work going, while departing cargo vehicles clear accumulated waste.

STORY 05 · New York · Holiday travel

The holiday road pause extends through Monday morning

New York has suspended lane closures for road and bridge construction on state highways from 6 a.m. November 25 until 6 a.m. Monday, November 30, to ease Thanksgiving travel. Emergency repairs and some work behind permanent concrete barriers can continue, so the pause does not mean every route is free of work zones. The state is also increasing enforcement against impaired and reckless driving through Sunday, November 29, and points travelers to 511NY for current road conditions. Together, the measures address two different pressures on the return trip: construction bottlenecks and the behavior that can turn heavy traffic into a dangerous journey.
