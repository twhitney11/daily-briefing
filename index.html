STORY 01 · SUNY · Workforce education

Short-term medical training gains a new route to Pell aid

SUNY Ulster’s Certified Clinical Medical Assistant program has become New York’s first program approved for Workforce Pell funding, SUNY announced November 20. The designation opens federal grant support to eligible students pursuing short-term training, with approved programs required to meet completion and employment benchmarks and connect to further degree study. For adults balancing work and family, the development makes a shorter route into health care more financially accessible, while other New York programs remain under consideration.

STORY 02 · AI · Research

A smaller model could handle an agent’s routine decisions

A new preprint introduces AnyS1, which turns written procedural skills into decision points that a four-billion-parameter model can handle before calling on a larger model when confidence is low. Across three unseen benchmarks, the authors report preserving task reliability while reducing per-task cost by 75–91 percent and model-call time by 63–92 percent compared with their larger-model baseline. The practical promise is cheaper automation through better assignment of work, though these are research results rather than independently verified savings for deployed systems.

STORY 03 · Cybersecurity · Enterprise IT

An Expressway flaw moves from patch notice to exploitation warning

Canada’s Cyber Centre reports that CISA added Cisco Expressway vulnerability CVE-2026-76466 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 20. Cisco’s October advisory describes a flaw in clustered Expressway-C and Expressway-E systems that can let an attacker with network access and persistent cookies execute commands as root. Cisco provides fixed software and says there is no workaround, making the newer exploitation notice a reason for administrators to revisit any unfinished rollout of the earlier patch.

STORY 04 · Space · Scientific instruments

A Mercury-bound telescope checks its vision against a distant nebula

The University of Leicester has released the first astronomical X-ray observations from BepiColombo’s MIXS instrument, using September views of the Crab Nebula to check its performance after eight years in space. The team found slightly sharper angular resolution than in its 2015 laboratory measurements, while noting that some instrument effects still need investigation and adjustment. MIXS is intended to map Mercury’s surface composition through fluorescent X-rays, so this calibration is a step toward interpreting the planet’s chemistry accurately. The release scheduled orbital insertion for November 21 and science operations for April 2027; it does not confirm that arrival has occurred.

STORY 05 · New York · Public services

Airport waiting time becomes an opening for driver health care

New York City has announced Driver Health Hubs at JFK and LaGuardia airports, with the first opening at JFK on Monday, November 23. The weekly hubs will offer rotating services for TLC-licensed drivers, including screenings, vaccinations, insurance enrollment assistance and same-day doctor appointments, alongside a mobile van serving other busy areas. Putting care where drivers wait for passengers addresses a practical barrier to preventive services: time away from the wheel can mean lost income.
