## Image decisions

it, retained the original and encoded WebP using scripts/optimize_image.py.
Visible label, alt text, rights statement, dimensions and hashes are recorded in
the edition and asset manifest. The imagery is not documentary or a technical
reconstruction. The botanical element is generic, not a cannabis identification.

Article images omitted rather than copying unlicensed assets: Geneseo credits
Damon Kimbrough without an explicit reuse grant; Binghamton credits Pexels without
an identified original/licensing record; Baylor's lead image is iStock/Getty;
ScienceDaily's generated art is controlled by that publisher. No permission was
inferred from public availability or from OCM's government status. No third-party
image was downloaded or embedded. No paid APIs or new credentials were used.
